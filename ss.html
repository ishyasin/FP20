How to use FP10SS Manager

Stack Counter counts a loose stack of unknown size.

1
Enter the department / FP10 code and the serial numbers of the first three sheets at the top of the stack, in order.
2
Enter the serial number of the last sheet at the bottom of the stack and click Count sheets.
3
Click Record & download log. The PDF has a Pharmacy Copy and a Ward Copy on one page.

Pack Down splits a full box (usually 2000) into equal packs in one session.

1
Check the box size and choose a pack size. Only sizes that divide the box exactly are offered.
2
Enter the first three serials from the top of the box and the serial of the last sheet in the box. The system confirms the box really holds that many sheets.
3
Leave Same cost centre for all packs ticked and enter one code, or untick it and enter a code for each pack in the table.
4
Click Calculate packs, check the ranges, then Record session & get paperwork to download the session log and a booking-out sheet for every pack.

If the fields turn red, check the numbers were entered in order and that the last serial belongs to the same batch.
