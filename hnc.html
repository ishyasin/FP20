How to use FP10HNC Manager

Each register is one physical FP10HNC pad. Have the pad in front of you when entering serial numbers.
1
Add a register
Click + Add register. Enter the department FP10 code, the number of prescriptions in the pad (usually 50) and the first three serial numbers from the front of the pad. The fields turn green when the pattern is recognised.
2
Add more pads
After each register the serial fields clear, ready for the next pad. The cost centre is fixed for the whole session after the first register.
3
End the session
Click End session. The session is recorded and you can download each register (cover sheet + tracking register) and the pharmacy session log.

The session is not saved until you click End session. Closing the page before then loses it.
