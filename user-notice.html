User notice

FP10 Manager 2 is developed and owned by ED&G™ (Evolve Digital and Governance). It is provided to authorised organisations for managing FP10HNC and FP10SS controlled stationery.

Authorised use

Access is for staff given a User ID by their organisation's administrator. Do not share your User ID or passcode. Every record you create is logged under your name.

Your responsibility

The tool calculates serial ranges and sheet counts from the numbers you enter. Always check the printed paperwork against the physical forms and follow your organisation's controlled stationery procedures, including wet-ink signatures where required.

Data

The tool records FP10 serial ranges, sheet counts, cost centres, and the name of the person who created each record. It does not record patient information.

Ownership

The software, its design and its calculation methods remain the property of ED&G™. You must not copy, reverse-engineer or redistribute any part of it.
